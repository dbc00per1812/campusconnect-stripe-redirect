Community Guidelines

Effective Date: September 22, 2025
Last Updated: November 12, 2025

Welcome to CampusLink!

Here is additional guidance on what is acceptable and unacceptable at CampusLink Labs, LLC. These guidelines help ensure a safe, respectful, and productive marketplace for all members of our campus community.

Core Community Rules

1. Language Standards

No vulgar language is tolerated on marketplace listings.

Keep your listings professional and respectful. This includes product descriptions, titles, and any communication within the platform.

2. Prohibited Content

Explicit or suggestive posts relating to the sale of substances including but not limited to alcohol, drugs or any illegal activities will not be tolerated and may result in legal ramifications.

CampusLink is for legitimate goods and services only. Listings that violate this rule will be immediately removed and may result in account termination and reporting to appropriate authorities.

3. Dispute Policy

If a user is involved in more than one payment dispute, they will be removed from the platform.

We take transaction integrity seriously. Multiple disputes indicate a pattern of problematic behavior and will result in permanent account suspension.

4. Communication & Availability

Respond in a timely manner to those interested in your listing. If a listing is no longer available for sale, please remove the post.

Good communication is essential for a thriving marketplace. Update your listings promptly and respond to inquiries within 24 hours when possible.

5. Service Arrangements

For certain services like tutoring, communicate to find a time that works for both members, and is in a safe public place on campus.

Always meet in well-lit, public areas. Never share your home address or meet in isolated locations. Safety first!

6. Service Quality

For tutoring services, tutors should make sure they are familiar with the relevant subject material beforehand.

Only offer services you're qualified to provide. Misrepresenting your skills or qualifications is a violation of these guidelines.

Content Standards

Pornography, Sex and Nudity

Pornography is difficult to define, but we know it when we see it, and we do not permit it on our Site. Most nudity and sexual content is not allowed. If content is intended to be sexually provocative, it is unacceptable.

Hate Speech

"Hate speech" refers to content that promotes hatred against members of a protected group. For instance, racist or sexist content may be considered hate speech. It is generally okay to criticize ideas or institutions, but not okay to make insulting generalizations about people based on their race, religion, gender, nationality, or other protected characteristics.

Shocking and Disgusting Content

It's not okay to post violent or gory content that's primarily intended to be shocking, sensational or disrespectful. Content should be appropriate for a diverse campus community.

Dangerous or Illegal Acts

We draw the line at content that's intended to incite violence or encourage dangerous, illegal activities that have an inherent risk of serious physical harm or death. This includes any content that promotes or facilitates illegal activities.

Children

Content involving children (anyone under the age of 18) are particularly sensitive. Such content should never be sexually suggestive or violent. Since CampusLink requires users to be 18+, content involving minors should be limited and appropriate.

Copyright & Trademarks

When you create something original, you own the copyright or possibly a trademark for it. Likewise, when other people create content, they may have a copyright or trademark to it. As a creative community, it's essential that everyone on our Site respect the copyrights and trademarks of others.

Best Practice: If you're not sure if something will violate someone's copyright, the safest thing to do is to create something completely original. If it's all yours, you never have to worry about copyrights or trademark rights.

Privacy

If a photo or video you've posted features people who are readily identifiable and who haven't consented to being featured, there's a chance they'll file a privacy complaint seeking its removal. Don't post other people's personal information, including phone numbers, addresses, credit card numbers, and government IDs.

We're serious about keeping our users safe and suspend accounts that violate people's privacy.

Harassment

It comes down to respect. CampusLink is all about sharing and interacting with the community in respectful ways. If you're not sure whether a comment or message crosses the line, follow a simple rule of thumb: if you wouldn't say it to someone's face, don't say it on CampusLink.

If you're looking to attack, harass, demean, or impersonate others, go elsewhere.

Impersonation

Impersonating someone by copying that person's profile, using a similar username, or posing as that person in comments or messages is considered impermissible. Please avoid any form of impersonation or harassing activity on the site.

Threats

Users shouldn't feel threatened when they're on our Site. Period. Don't make threatening comments or send threatening messages to other users. Any threats of violence will result in immediate account suspension and may be reported to law enforcement.

Marketplace-Specific Rules

Listing Quality

  • Use clear, accurate photos of items you're selling
  • Provide honest, complete descriptions
  • Price items fairly and accurately
  • Update or remove sold items promptly

Transaction Conduct

  • Honor your commitments to buy or sell
  • Communicate clearly about meetup times and locations
  • Be on time for scheduled exchanges
  • Treat items with care if trying before buying

Safety Best Practices

  • Meet in public, well-lit campus locations
  • Consider bringing a friend to exchanges
  • Trust your instincts - if something feels off, it probably is
  • Report suspicious behavior or listings immediately

Consequences for Violations

By using CampusLink, you agree to follow these Community Guidelines. Violations may result in:

  • Content removal
  • Temporary account suspension
  • Permanent account removal from the platform
  • Reporting to university administration
  • Reporting to law enforcement (for illegal activities)

Reporting Violations

If you see content or behavior that violates these guidelines, please report it immediately using the in-app reporting feature or by contacting us at:

Email: support@campuslink.app
Subject Line: Community Guidelines Violation

We review all reports and take appropriate action to maintain a safe, respectful community for everyone.

Thank you for being a responsible member of the CampusLink community!
